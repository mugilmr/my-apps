RENT ROLL - SHARED DATA WITH A GOOGLE SHEET
===========================================
Your Google account owns the Sheet. The landlord and anyone else only need
the app link and a passcode. They do NOT need a Google account.

PART 1 - THE GOOGLE SHEET (about 10 minutes, on a laptop)
  1. Go to https://sheets.google.com and create a Blank spreadsheet.
     Name it "Rent Roll Data".
  2. Menu: Extensions > Apps Script. A code editor opens.
  3. Delete everything in the editor. Open google-sheet/Code.gs from this
     folder in any text editor, copy ALL of it, paste it into the editor.
  4. Near the top, change CHANGE-THIS-PASSCODE to your own passcode:
       const PASSCODE = 'CHANGE-THIS-PASSCODE';
     Use 10 or more letters and digits. Example: 'Kumar-Rent-4821'. Do not
     use spaces. Remember it: you and the landlord type it on each phone.
  5. Click the Save icon (disk). Name the project "Rent Roll".
  6. Click Deploy (top right) > New deployment.
     - Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
     - Description: Rent Roll
     - Execute as: Me (your email)
     - Who has access: Anyone
     - Click Deploy.
  7. Click "Authorize access", choose your Google account. If you see
     "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced > "Go to Rent Roll
     (unsafe)" > Allow. This is your own script, so it is safe.
  8. Copy the "Web app" URL. It ends with /exec. Keep it.

PART 2 - PUT THE URL IN THE APP (GitHub, 3 minutes)
  1. Open your GitHub repository (my-apps) > open the rent folder.
  2. Upload this release's files over the old ones (drag the contents of
     the extracted rent folder into the repository's rent folder). Also
     upload the google-sheet folder if you want to keep a copy there.
  3. Open rent/sheets-config.js on GitHub, click the pencil (edit), and
     paste your URL between the quotes:
        url: "https://script.google.com/macros/s/XXXXXXXX/exec"
     Click "Commit changes".
  4. Wait 1 to 2 minutes for GitHub Pages to update.
  IMPORTANT: Never put the passcode in any file on GitHub. The repository
  is public. The passcode is only typed into the phone.

PART 3 - OPEN IT ON THE PHONES
  1. On your iPhone, open in Safari:
        https://naveenragav2005.github.io/my-apps/rent/
  2. A box asks for the passcode. Type it and tap Continue.
  3. You will see the landlord's 10 properties and 23 units already loaded
     with their rents. The pill at the top should say "Saved and shared".
  4. Share > Add to Home Screen > Add. Open it from the icon.
  5. Send the landlord the same link and the passcode. He opens it in
     Safari, types the passcode, and adds it to his Home Screen too.
     Anything either of you enters appears on the other phone within about
     15 seconds (open the app or pull it to the front to refresh sooner).

PART 4 - FIRST THINGS TO DO IN THE APP
  1. Check each property's rent on the Properties page. The starter data
     assumes rent is due on the 5th and is tracked from the current month.
  2. For each unit open "Edit dates and details" and enter the real tenant
     name, phone number, move-in date, advance (optional) and due day.
  3. If someone still owes rent from before you started using the app:
     open the unit > "Add earlier pending" and enter the amount and month.
  4. Each month: tap Mark paid, or Record payment for part payments,
     multi-month payments, or payments split across months.
  5. In the Google Sheet you will see four readable tabs, refreshed a few
     seconds after changes: This month, Pending, Payments, Tenants.
     The "Data" tab is the app's storage. Never edit it by hand.

SECURITY
  - Share the Google Sheet with nobody, or only as Viewer. If someone can
    edit the Data tab they can damage the data.
  - Anyone who has the passcode can read and change all rent data. Treat it
    like a bank PIN. To change it: edit PASSCODE in Apps Script, then
    Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil > Version: New version > Deploy.
    Then tap Settings > Change passcode on each phone.
  - Passcode protection is simple. Do not store tenant ID numbers in the app.

BACKUPS
  - Settings > Back up or restore, once a month. Save the file to iCloud.
  - Sheet: File > Version history shows earlier states of the Data tab.

UPDATING THE SCRIPT LATER
  If a new Code.gs is ever provided: paste it over the old one, keep your
  passcode, then Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil > New version > Deploy.
  The URL stays the same.

KNOWN LIMITS
  - If two people edit the same month's payments at exactly the same moment,
    the later save wins. Rare for a single landlord with one helper.
  - If a phone is offline, its changes are kept and uploaded when it is
    online again. A property or tenant change made offline replaces the
    shared property list, so re-check recent changes made by others.
  - Google allows a generous but limited number of script runs per day.
    The app checks for changes every 15 seconds only while it is open.
